- **Primary category:** News · **Secondary:** Reference

## Description
Meridian is a live, map-first world-news reader. Every real event is a dot placed **where it actually
happened** — a strike on the city it hit, a summit in the capital that hosts it — never on the publisher's
country. Tap a dot for a clean, copyright-free brief in Meridian's own words, a relevant photo, and the actual
footage from the wire.

• A map of what's happening in the world, right now.
• Fast and plentiful — from the OSINT channels and wires that break news first.
• Every dot means something: no op-eds, no "week in pictures".
• Country desks: who's in charge today, the political landscape, and the last 24 hours there.
• Flat map or globe, light or dark.
